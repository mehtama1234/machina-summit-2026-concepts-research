High-Value Talks

Detailed first-principles writeups for the talks that best frame the summit: frontier robotics ambition and deployment reality.

Talk 9 · NVIDIA

Jim Fan / NVIDIA: Robotics - The End Game

The talk argues that robotics needs its own version of the language-model scaling stack: world-state prediction, action fine-tuning, scalable non-robot data, simulation environments, and agentic self-improvement loops.

Talk 36 · Field AI

Ali Agha / Field AI: Deployment Over Demos

The talk argues that physical AI succeeds only when it is reliable in dirty, dull, dangerous real environments, not merely impressive in controlled demos.

Talk 8 · Google DeepMind

Carolina Parada / Google DeepMind: Language to Motion

The talk explains how Gemini Robotics turns multimodal foundation models into physical agents by adding action as a model output, then layering planning, tools, memory, safety evaluation, and whole-body control around it.

Talk 18 · Lightwheel

Steve Xie / Lightwheel: Continuous Learning Systems for Physical AI

The talk argues that physical AI needs a continuous learning infrastructure built from human egocentric data, simulation evaluation, deployment feedback, and real-to-sim-to-real conversion.

Talk 19 · Stanford and Centific

Jiajun Wu / Stanford and Vasu Sundarababu / Centific: Physical AI's Data Gaps

The panel explains that physical AI's key bottleneck is not simply more data, but standardized, synchronized, multimodal, task-relevant data with reliable evaluation and expert validation.

Talk 34 · Rhoda AI

Andrew Wooten / Rhoda AI: World Action Models

The talk argues that deployable learning robots need world action models: models pretrained on internet-scale video to understand physics, then fine-tuned with robot trajectories to convert imagined task videos into real-time actions.
