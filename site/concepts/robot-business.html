Robot Business Models

Robot business models decide who pays, what workflow is improved, how reliability is guaranteed, and how value exceeds deployment cost.

Ordinary Problem

Robots can be technically impressive while failing to create a purchaseable, maintainable product.

Naive Picture

The naive view is that a polished demo or a larger model is enough to make the capability real.

Why It Fails

Robotics and agentic systems fail in integration: data, hardware, deployment, verification, safety, cost, and customer workflows all interact.

First-Principles Move

A robot company sells an operational outcome, not a robot demo. The economics must include uptime, integration, support, safety, and labor substitution or augmentation.

What Breaks Without It

Without this concept, it is easy to confuse a narrow prototype with a repeatable system that can operate in real environments.

Summit Role

This theme organizes summit talks related to robot business models and gives us a first-pass route for later deep dives.
