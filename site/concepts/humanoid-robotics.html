Humanoid Robotics

Humanoid robotics is the effort to build general-purpose robots shaped for human environments and work routines.

Ordinary Problem

Factories, warehouses, homes, and public spaces are built around human bodies, tools, and assumptions.

Naive Picture

The naive view is that a polished demo or a larger model is enough to make the capability real.

Why It Fails

Robotics and agentic systems fail in integration: data, hardware, deployment, verification, safety, cost, and customer workflows all interact.

First-Principles Move

If the environment is human-shaped, a robot with compatible mobility, manipulation, perception, and safety behavior can reuse existing infrastructure instead of requiring every workplace to be rebuilt.

What Breaks Without It

Without this concept, it is easy to confuse a narrow prototype with a repeatable system that can operate in real environments.

Summit Role

This theme organizes summit talks related to humanoid robotics and gives us a first-pass route for later deep dives.
