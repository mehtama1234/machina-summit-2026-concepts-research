physical-ai and robotics

Agentic AI Workflows

Agentic AI workflows use models and tools to perform multi-step work across software, documents, web data, and operations.

Ordinary Problem

Many valuable tasks require search, state, tool use, verification, and repeated decisions rather than one answer.

Naive Picture

The naive view is that a polished demo or a larger model is enough to make the capability real.

Why It Fails

Robotics and agentic systems fail in integration: data, hardware, deployment, verification, safety, cost, and customer workflows all interact.

First-Principles Move

An agentic workflow is a feedback loop: plan, act, observe, verify, and revise until a useful operational state is reached.

What Breaks Without It

Without this concept, it is easy to confuse a narrow prototype with a repeatable system that can operate in real environments.

Summit Role

This theme organizes summit talks related to agentic ai workflows and gives us a first-pass route for later deep dives.
