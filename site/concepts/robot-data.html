physical-ai and robotics

Robot Data And Learning

Robot data is the collection of demonstrations, sensor traces, simulations, failures, and field logs that teaches embodied systems what to do.

Ordinary Problem

Robotics lacks the internet-scale natural data advantage that language models had.

Naive Picture

The naive view is that a polished demo or a larger model is enough to make the capability real.

Why It Fails

Robotics and agentic systems fail in integration: data, hardware, deployment, verification, safety, cost, and customer workflows all interact.

First-Principles Move

A robot learns from trajectories, not just labels. Useful data must connect observations, actions, outcomes, and context.

What Breaks Without It

Without this concept, it is easy to confuse a narrow prototype with a repeatable system that can operate in real environments.

Summit Role

This theme organizes summit talks related to robot data and learning and gives us a first-pass route for later deep dives.
