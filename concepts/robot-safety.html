physical-ai and robotics

Robot Safety And Trust

Robot safety and trust cover the controls, validation, norms, and operational practices that make humans willing to share space and work with robots.

Ordinary Problem

Physical failures can damage property or harm people, so acceptable error rates are much lower than in software-only systems.

Naive Picture

The naive view is that a polished demo or a larger model is enough to make the capability real.

Why It Fails

Robotics and agentic systems fail in integration: data, hardware, deployment, verification, safety, cost, and customer workflows all interact.

First-Principles Move

Safety is a systems property: perception, motion constraints, fail-safes, human factors, monitoring, and accountability must work together.

What Breaks Without It

Without this concept, it is easy to confuse a narrow prototype with a repeatable system that can operate in real environments.

Summit Role

This theme organizes summit talks related to robot safety and trust and gives us a first-pass route for later deep dives.
