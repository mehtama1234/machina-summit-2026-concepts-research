physical-ai and robotics

Robot Hardware And Components

Robot hardware includes the actuators, sensors, compute, batteries, materials, and manufacturing systems that turn AI into embodied work.

Ordinary Problem

A clever policy cannot overcome weak actuators, poor sensing, fragile mechanisms, or uneconomic manufacturing.

Naive Picture

The naive view is that a polished demo or a larger model is enough to make the capability real.

Why It Fails

Robotics and agentic systems fail in integration: data, hardware, deployment, verification, safety, cost, and customer workflows all interact.

First-Principles Move

Physical capability is bounded by hardware: force, precision, endurance, cost, repairability, and manufacturability.

What Breaks Without It

Without this concept, it is easy to confuse a narrow prototype with a repeatable system that can operate in real environments.

Summit Role

This theme organizes summit talks related to robot hardware and components and gives us a first-pass route for later deep dives.
